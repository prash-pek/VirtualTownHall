No item rejected. Corrections fall in three groups:
1. **Pronouns.** Pamphlet statements are written in the first person and no candidate states pronouns, so statements were reworded with the surname. Kept he/his for Moberg (his campaign site uses "his") and she/her for Tourville (her pamphlet statement uses "she").
2. **Excerpt shorter than the statement.** Excerpts were extended with words that appear in order in the candidate's own pamphlet column (the two-column extract interleaves lines). Examples: Reyes b1 (dropped "seeking a third", not in the excerpt), Dittman b1, Duren b2, Tourville b3.
3. **Mislabelled sources.** Tualatin Life pieces by Gonzalez, Pratt, Reyes and Brooks are bylined candidate writing, but the pages do not call them "guest letters/columns". Relabelled "candidate-written, bylined" (Pratt's is titled "A Letter to Tualatin").

Reyes lists none.
- **Web sources.** All re-fetched and excerpts present: Gonzalez site /priorities (3), Dittman site /priorities, Moberg site /aboutdean and /swcd-priorities, Tourville site /about-me, the Governor's 2026-07-17 release, and the four Tualatin Life articles. The Governor's release says her appointment is effective immediately.
- **Websites.** Gonzalez (Octavio4Tualatin.com), Dittman, Moberg and Tourville domains are printed in their pamphlet statements. Pratt, Reyes, Brooks, Duren, Graham and VanderZanden have no site (documented gaps).

Gonzalez is a sitting councilor running for mayor, so incumbent=false for the mayor race is right. Pratt and Dittman are false. The SWCD board page lists Moberg (At-Large #2) and Duren (Zone 4), so both true. Graham and VanderZanden are not on the board page (false). Zone 5 is currently held by Luke VanderZanden, who is not on the ballot. Tourville is true (appointed 2026-07-17).
